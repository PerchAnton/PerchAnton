## 👋 About Me

I'm an entrepreneur and a PhD student in **Automation, Computer-Integrated Technologies and Robotics** at the **National Technical University "Dnipro Polytechnic"** in Ukraine.

I work on industrial process modeling, control systems, and practical software projects for research and business. I enjoy turning ideas into useful tools and exploring new ways to solve real-world problems.

## 🔬 Research & Automation

My research focuses on the **modeling and automatic control of crushing and transfer complexes**, with an emphasis on **energy efficiency** and **throughput optimization**.

| Area | Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| ⚙️ Industrial automation | Control systems for industrial processes |
| 📐 Mathematical modeling | Understanding and simulating process behavior |
| ⚡ Process optimization | Improving energy efficiency and productivity |

## 💻 Software & Projects

I work on practical software tools for engineering research and business workflows. I also use **AI-assisted development** to explore ideas, experiment with solutions, and build useful applications.

My interests include industrial automation, mathematical modeling, process optimization, and business software.
